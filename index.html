A snapshot of every pool is saved to this repo each day, so this chart fills in as the days pass. Moves between pools show up here as one band growing while another shrinks.

What this measures

Net flow is the day-to-day change in the combined size of all shielded pools (Sprout, Sapling, Orchard and Ironwood). A move from one pool to another cancels out, so a migration between pools does not show up as an inflow or outflow. Only ZEC entering or leaving the shielded pools does. It cannot show who moved the coins, because shielded transactions are private by design. The newest day may still be filling in.

Data from CipherScan’s public API, collected daily by a GitHub Action. Open data: daily_totals.csv and pool_snapshots.csv.
